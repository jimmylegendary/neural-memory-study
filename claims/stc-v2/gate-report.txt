7. WHAT THE GATE DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOES  — every claim carries >=1 admissible, typed, resolvable <path>@<commit> pointer;
        no generated/prose text is used as evidence; at least one gate-PASSED claim
        references a result block that carries content (require_results, harness.py:157);
        no P3 patent-interlock is outstanding.
DOES NOT — resolve ref CONTENT. gate.py is explicit that "does that commit contain that
        file" is the Source adapter's seam, not the gate's. That is exactly the hole
        claims/stc-v2/verify_bundle.py closes: it git-cat-file's all 22 distinct refs at
        the anchor commit 927a8b50, re-resolves all 159 JSON pointers, and byte-compares
        every transcribed number and string. Both had to run; only both together mean
        anything.
ALSO NOT — whether a statement is TRUE of its source. That is the discipline's job (the
        §/p locators on every P2 evidence entry are what makes it checkable by a human),
        and the honesty contract in provenance_manifest.note is what bounds how the P1
        numbers may be used in prose.

KNOWN DOWNSTREAM NIT (in veridraft, not in this bundle). Metric names here are exact JSON
pointers, and 4 of the 50 contain a literal "|" because the experiment's own key is
`flops(w_in=1.0),|c_hat|/|c|=4.0` / `omega=0.0|replay=none`. veridraft/core/assemble.py:114
interpolates metric names straight into a markdown table row without escaping "|", so those
4 rows would render with extra columns in the generated experimental_log.md. The fix belongs
in assemble.py (escape cell content); mangling the pointer in the bundle to suit a renderer
would break the byte-compare that makes the numbers checkable. Not a gate condition — the
gate ran and passed; `assemble` was not run.
