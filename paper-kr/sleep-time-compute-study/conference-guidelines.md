# Conference manuscript editorial contract

- Target: 12–18 A4 pages including references.
- Audience: ML systems, continual-learning, agent-memory, and device/system researchers.
- Source freeze: 2026-08-05; only primary papers, official releases/docs, and tagged repositories are load-bearing.
- Narrative spine: definition → evidence/strongest alternatives → conditional verdict → scaling hypotheses → infrastructure/device implications → falsifiable benchmark → limitations.
- Public boundary: only `public=true,status=supported` claims may appear as release claims. P3 device hypotheses remain patent-first held; the paper exposes workload/evaluation questions only.
- Scaling language: identity, analytical break-even, fit candidate, and hypothesis must remain visibly distinct. No universal sleep-FLOP law is asserted.
- Comparison rule: no fixed-model straw man; match long context/RAG, temporal external memory, periodic refresh, editing, PEFT, and wake TTT by complete lifecycle resources.
- Page-budget cuts: exhaustive chronology, full derivations, extended benchmark design, negative-evidence matrix, and canonical claim ledger live in the separate Appendix.
- Reproducibility: manuscript SHA, source registry, claim map, figure ledger, build manifest, QA, overflow scan, and visual-review artifact must ship together.
